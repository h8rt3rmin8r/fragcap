# S173 data model

## Recipient

Existing OutputRecipient retains exact recipient SID, producer SID, proof kind and proven ordinary token/session. Elevated desktop acquisition uses session-shell proof after exact identity and ordinary elevation/integrity validation. No serialized handle or new persistent field.

## Failure and verification

Acquisition failure has an operation-stage label and original platform error. Existing actual per-path enumeration/read results and bounded limits remain independent of completeness and cleanup. Only verified final access permits accessible-output guidance.

## Retirement

Inspection/proposal/repair report types leave the public facade and CLI. No bundle is migrated or modified. Collection/recovery authority and explicit recipient handoff retain their state transitions.
