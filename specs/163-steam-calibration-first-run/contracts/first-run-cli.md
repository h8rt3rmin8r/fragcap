# First-run CLI contract

`fragcap calibrate <target>` starts target resolution. If the Steam target has no authored client, human mode explains that the Steam executable is a launch hint and offers a separately confirmed, read-only owned-launch observation. A unique socket-owning descendant may be proposed under a separate exact client-authoring plan; that evidence does not claim captured traffic. A manual declaration is labeled as an operator choice. JSON and `--authorize-stdin` retain exact plan-ID behavior and never treat a bare `y` as authorization there. Setup ends after an authored change, allowing the operator to close the title normally before a fresh cold calibration.

`fragcap targets show <target>` prints the active ordered launch entries with roles and the Steam launch hint under separate labels. Machine-readable export retains its existing schema and values.

After a delegated calibration session, human output states the earliest known failed stage, observed retention and proxy counts, exact compatibility fact writes, workflow state and next action, and one complete cleanup summary. Unknown observations are called unavailable. Structured events remain unchanged unless a versioned contract is explicitly amended during implementation.

No command accepts a heuristic Steam hint as directly observed socket ownership. A separate authorized Deep Capture plan is still required after client setup.
