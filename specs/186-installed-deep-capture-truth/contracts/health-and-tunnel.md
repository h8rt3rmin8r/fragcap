# Contract: Installation health and HTTPS tunnel truth

## Installation health

Read-only health determines public identity, receipt, validity, and exact `LocalMachine/Root` readback. Protected signer accessibility is a separate observation. Access denied on the protected signer during ordinary startup yields signer proof `unverified` and no repair recommendation when public health is valid. A confirmed absent or malformed key remains a specific failure. Deep Capture still performs fresh exact trust and sign/verify before effects; failure stops the attempt.

## HTTPS tunnel

An origin-form inner request under CONNECT uses HTTPS as its scheme and 443 as the default Host port. Explicit Host port overrides the default. Equivalent normalized authorities may share the verified CONNECT upstream. Genuine distinct authority must either establish separately scoped and verified upstream identity with complete evidence or return a precise refusal; it cannot reuse the CONNECT upstream under another Host. The parser retains the original request bytes and does not silently rewrite evidence.

## Terminal accounting

Authority refusal and header timeout are separate terminal codes with observed stage. A six-connection controlled pattern of five refusals and one timeout has no full HTTPS result. A complete verified response is required before full HTTPS inspectability or HAR completion can be reported.
