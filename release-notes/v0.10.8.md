# Highlights

- Capture now honors elapsed duration, selected roles and directions, and metadata-only pcapng headers. Output paths can include literal commas.
- Managed Deep Capture can close exact session-owned applications after a confirmed, bounded shutdown plan. Doctor preserves a failed cleanup result after the resources themselves become readable again.
- First-use guidance now identifies missing elevation, gives a same-store next step after Steam client setup, and explains required Deep Capture launch and sensitive-output controls.
- CLI actions give more precise previews and failure guidance. Global `--json` results are typed across target, catalog, schema, technology, extcap administration and bundle commands; `--silent` suppresses optional human output while retaining required results.
- The locked rustls-webpki update rejects the distrusted intermediate keys covered by RUSTSEC-2026-0382.

For the complete change history, implementation details, and issue references, see the [full v0.10.8 changelog](https://github.com/h8rt3rmin8r/fragcap/blob/v0.10.8/CHANGELOG.md).
