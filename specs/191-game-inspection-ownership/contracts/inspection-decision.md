# Contract: Native Inspection Decision

The native proxy accepts an injected, thread-safe provider with no CLI or Windows dependency. The provider receives a connection or association descriptor and a finite deadline, then returns exactly one immutable typed decision. The producer may use live Windows socket and process evidence behind that interface. No socket enumeration or process-state lock is held on the accept loop.

`launch-chain` resolves to `inspect` after normal authentication and destination admission, preserving current behavior. `exact-game` resolves to `inspect` only on exact selected-game owner proof. Known middleware and every unresolved owner resolve to `uninspected` with a stable reason. A result that arrives after the deadline cannot authorize inspection. #559 supplies blind byte forwarding for the `uninspected` action; #561 exposes the operator selection.

The decision is made before client-facing leaf creation, TLS accept, content retention, and key-log registration. The proxy records decision identity and reason without retaining credentials or private process paths in public diagnostics. Admission and destination policy remain separately enforced.
