# Release handoff contract: S164

The candidate handoff reports the official PR URL, final commit, required CI status, review dispositions, unresolved threads, and exact candidate scope. It states explicitly that owner merge, tag, workflow publication, public verification, and published-state reconciliation remain pending.

After owner merge and separate publication authorization, the publication record must bind the tag to exact merged source, all four release jobs, normal owner approval if requested, six public files and three matching checksum sidecars, certification report, and ten non-yanked crates with checksums. Only verified facts may move current published-state markers. A pending, missing, failed, or contradictory field prevents a completion claim.
