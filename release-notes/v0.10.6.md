# Highlights

- Doctor condenses healthy session history while keeping actionable recovery and exact details visible. Discovery and calibration reports separate readiness, attempted results, connection causes, artifacts and cleanup.
- Deep Capture keeps required loopback acquisition with selected interfaces and improves final-client packet correlation and missing-evidence explanations.
- Windows bundles retain access for the intended ordinary user, with explicit recipient handoff and exact historical permission inspection and repair.
- Generated operational history has finite retention. Preview-authorized collection reclaims eligible contents, and empty-container removal is a separate explicit action; saved and historical evidence retain their declared cleanup policy.
- Documentation and historical research dependencies include the accumulated security corrections.
