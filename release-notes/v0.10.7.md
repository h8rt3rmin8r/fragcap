# Highlights

- Elevated Deep Capture and calibration on Windows now leave new output readable from your normal desktop, without entering your account SID or opening a second terminal. Failures identify the Windows operation that failed.
- The historical bundle access-inspect and access-repair commands have been removed. Existing collection, sensitive cleanup, Doctor recovery and different-account authorization remain available; use Windows permission tools for inaccessible older output.
- Seven TLS and QUIC dependency advisories are corrected with minimal Rustls and quinn-proto patch updates.

For the complete change history, implementation details, and issue references, see the [full v0.10.7 changelog](https://github.com/h8rt3rmin8r/fragcap/blob/v0.10.7/CHANGELOG.md).
